Questions

POA services questions

What is a power of attorney (POA)?

A POA is a document that lets someone act for you. Its wording sets what they can and can’t do.

Can Keylee act for me if I’m abroad?

Yes, within the powers your POA gives us. We check it covers the task before we act.

What can a POA cover?

That depends on how it is written. We check your document against the task you need done.

Who prepares the POA?

The document itself goes through the proper channels. We help you navigate the steps and the paperwork. For advice on wording, speak to a qualified adviser.

How will I know what you did?

You get copies of every document and a record of every action.
